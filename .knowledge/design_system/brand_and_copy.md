# Brand and Copy

> Keep the Openings identity and product language specific, human, and verifiable.

## Logo

The canonical identity is the exact user-supplied stacked-page symbol and `openings.dev` lockup. The full artwork is used in the header, footer, README, and design-system showcase. The stacked-page symbol cropped from the same vector is the compact favicon and small-space mark.

Render the lockup without a container, outline, hard shadow, reconstructed typography, or decorative companion mark. Keep every part monochrome, preserve the submitted proportions, and generate light, dark, and compact variants from `components/brand/geometry.ts` through `npm run brand:generate`.

## Positioning

Openings makes technology jobs already shared through public GitHub communities easier to find and evaluate. Every result remains linked to its original public source.

The approved English thesis is “Find tech jobs shared by GitHub communities.” Portuguese adapts it as “Encontre as vagas de tecnologia que as comunidades já estão publicando.” Other locales receive native rewrites, not literal syntax transfers.

## Voice

- Lead with the candidate’s concrete change.
- Explain the GitHub-community mechanism immediately after the promise.
- Use short, natural sentences and specific nouns.
- Keep community context human without romanticizing it.
- Name the action that follows.
- Use counts, dates, and provenance only when supplied by real data.

Avoid hype, false urgency, invented trust claims, repeated marketing formulas, AI clichés, generic future language, and prose that sounds translated.

## CTA vocabulary

- `Search open jobs`
- `Search jobs`
- `Open original listing`
- `See open jobs`
- `Browse communities`
- `Share profile`
- `Clear filters`

Use a more precise action instead of `Learn more`, `Get started`, or `Explore` whenever the destination is known.

## Claims boundary

Openings indexes and presents public listings. It does not verify employers, guarantee accuracy, rank candidate fit, operate the application process, or own the listing. Copy and metadata must not imply those capabilities.

The internal `publisher` presentation kind is derived from the GitHub account recorded as the listing author. Public copy describes that factual GitHub author naturally in each language and never implies a verified business relationship. Community and author country/region values summarize indexed job locations; never present them as the real location of a person or community.

Visible copy, placeholders, accessible labels, empty states, and errors live in the typed locale dictionaries. Review meaning and rhythm independently in English, Portuguese, Spanish, Italian, French, and German.
